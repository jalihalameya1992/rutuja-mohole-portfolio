i've spent the last ten-plus years doing one thing mostly: designing packaging that people actually pick up off the shelf.

Senior Communication Designer & Brand Design Strategist Elephant Design, Pune

Most of my work is FMCG: dairy, snacking, skincare, biscuits. In those aisles a pack has about two seconds to convince someone.

The job often starts before there's a name on anything and runs all the way to a system that has to work across dozens of SKUs and formats. I've worked on Saffola, Epigamia, WickedGud, Mother Dairy, Himalaya, ITC Sunfeast, Reliance Smart Bazaar, PetStar and Mahindra Car&Bike, and a few of them have won design awards.
