Epigamia Milkshakes

A packaging system for Epigamia's move from Greek yogurt into indulgent Ready-to-Drink milkshakes.

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Packaging design + System design
Category
Ready-to-drink dairy, millennial-focused

Task at hand

Epigamia, a clean-label name in Indian dairy, wanted to go beyond Greek yogurt into ready-to-drink milkshakes. The pack had to feel indulgent, aimed at millennials, and premium enough to justify a higher price, while speaking to the "Chhotu Bhook" craving in a category full of functional, mass-market flavoured milk.

Challenge

Sell the milkshakes on creamy, unapologetic indulgence rather than the usual clinical milk benefits. The idea was built around one line, "Now You Can Drink The Ice-Cream!", so the range stands out on shelf and on screen.

In-store fridge activation.

02 / 09 the system

Design System

A colour system built around flavour first, then stretched out across everything from the fridge door to the side of a delivery truck.

Colour blocking Matte pastel backgrounds: soft lilac for Vanilla, blush pink for Strawberry, warm cocoa for Chocolate. A black header band carries the brand colour.
Simple hero frames A circular frame in the middle holds a cream splash and real ingredients (vanilla pods, ripe strawberries, chocolate slabs) so the taste does the talking.
What you read first A clean grid keeps the flavour name front and centre and puts the nutrition claims in small stamps, so the pack stays premium.
Out in the world The system carried over to transit billboards, delivery trucks and flavour-led key visuals, so people recognise it from the street to the shelf.
