Himalaya Sheet Masks

A packaging system for Himalaya's global sheet mask launch, across five variants.

Elephant Design, Pune

🏆 A'Design Award & Competition: Silver, Packaging Design
Role
Packaging Design + System Design
Category
Global skincare, five variants

Task at hand

Design the packaging for Himalaya Sheet Masks, a new global skincare range that had to appeal to younger buyers without losing Himalaya's reputation for natural wellness. Five variants, sold in international markets.

Challenge

Stand out in a crowded global sheet mask category. Balance Himalaya's natural, science-backed heritage with a youthful, trend-led look that works across different cultures, and make it hold up across sachets, cartons and retail displays.

05 / 09 the system

Design System

A sheet mask can feel clinical. We made it read more like a five-minute treat than a routine.

A five-minute pause We framed the sheet mask as a self-care ritual, with bright, uplifting visuals that make skincare feel calm and enjoyable.
Ingredients, drawn Illustrated herbs, fruits and botanicals give the packs a natural, premium feel and point to what each mask does.
Young, bright, chatty Bright colours, playful type and chatty copy help the range speak to Gen Z and younger skincare buyers.
One look, five variants A shared layout with a colour for each variant makes the range easy to shop and keeps it looking like one family.
The carton is the display The pack graphics and the outer carton work together as a ready-made retail display, which helps with logistics and shelf visibility.
