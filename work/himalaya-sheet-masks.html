Himalaya Sheet Masks

Elephant Design, Pune

🏆 A'Design Award & Competition — Silver, Packaging Design
Role
Packaging design + System design
Category
Global skincare, five variants

Task at hand

Design the packaging system for Himalaya Sheet Masks, a new global skincare range that would appeal to younger consumers while preserving Himalaya's trusted legacy of natural wellness. The goal was to create a cohesive visual system across five variants for international markets.

Challenge

Enter a highly cluttered global sheet mask category with a distinctive visual system. Balance Himalaya's heritage of natural, science-backed skincare with a youthful, disruptive trend-led aesthetic — resonating across diverse global markets without alienating different cultures, at a scalable architecture across sachets, cartons, and retail displays.

05 / 07 the system

Design System

Turned a sheet mask — a fairly clinical thing — into something that reads more like a five-minute treat than a routine.

Zen time concept Reimagined sheet masks as a self-care ritual, using a vibrant, uplifting visual language that transforms skincare into a calming, enjoyable experience.
Illustrative ingredient storytelling Romanticized illustrations of herbs, fruits, and botanicals create a premium, natural, contemporary aesthetic while highlighting product benefits.
Youthful visual language Bright colour palettes, playful typography, and conversational messaging help the range connect with Gen Z and young skincare consumers.
Unified variant architecture A cohesive layout with colour-coded variants ensures easy navigation while maintaining a strong family identity across the range.
Integrated display system Packaging graphics and secondary carton function together as an efficient retail display, optimizing logistics and shelf visibility.
