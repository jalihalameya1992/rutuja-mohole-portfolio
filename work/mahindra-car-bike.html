Mahindra Car&Bike

Rebranding Mahindra First Choice into car&bike, a marketplace for pre-owned cars.

Elephant Design, Pune

🏆 e4m India Design Excellence Awards: Gold, Best Design for Rebranding
Role
Identity Design + Visual System Guidelines + Retail Design
Scale
450+ cities, 1,100+ stores, 2M+ customers

Task at hand

Mahindra First Choice was a trusted editorial voice on used cars. People read it, but they didn't go there to buy. The job was to turn it into a real marketplace, car&bike, with one identity that works from a mobile app to a showroom facade, and to keep the editorial trust that got it there.

Challenge

Selling cars without losing the editorial objectivity people trusted. Building one system that stretches from mobile UI to signage, retail interiors and regional languages. And standing out against aggressive, digital-native used-car aggregators while keeping the brand's credibility.

The car&bike Superstore, as built.

09 / 09 the system

Design System

The name already had the idea in it. "car&bike" gave us the ampersand as the heart of the mark.

The ampersand as identity We built the rebrand around matchmaking. The "&" became the core of the logo, standing in for the brand's job of connecting buyers and sellers.
Evolution, not reinvention The change tells customers Mahindra's legacy is still underneath. We kept the brand equity and updated how it looks.
Buy, sell, exchange The brand line shows up at every touchpoint. Wayfinding towers, signage and retail interiors all lead with the same three words.
Nine scripts deep Worked with a type designer to rebuild the logo lockup across nine regional scripts, so it reads consistently from a metro showroom to a small-town superstore.
One system, every touchpoint Guidelines cover app UI, signage, showroom interiors, vehicles and print, and are built to work across 1,100+ stores in 450+ cities.
