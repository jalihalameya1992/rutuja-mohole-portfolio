Mother Dairy Beverages

One packaging system for Mother Dairy's milkshakes, lassi and buttermilk range.

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Packaging Design + System Design
Category
Milkshakes, lassi, buttermilk

Task at hand

Create the packaging identity for Mother Dairy's beverage portfolio — milkshakes, lassi, and buttermilk — by building a unified design system that modernizes the range while preserving the trust and familiarity of the Mother Dairy brand. The objective: a scalable architecture across multiple formats, flavors, and consumer occasions, relevant to modern wellness-conscious families while maintaining instant recognition across retail aisles.

Challenge

Create a cohesive visual identity spanning indulgent milkshakes and traditional dairy drinks. Appeal to younger consumers without alienating Mother Dairy's existing audience. Maintain consistency across cartons, pouches, and bottles while ensuring clear variant and category differentiation — balancing premium appetite appeal with functional communication in a fast-moving retail environment.

04 / 09 the system

Design System

One system had to hold three fairly different drinks — milkshakes, lassi, buttermilk — without losing the thread between them.

Distinct yet unified A common packaging architecture unifies Milkshakes, Lassi, and Buttermilk under the Mother Dairy masterbrand while giving each category its own visual character.
Appetite appeal Dynamic milk splashes and vibrant ingredient imagery communicate freshness, indulgence, and authentic flavour.
Clear hierarchy A strong information flow with prominent flavor cues, nutrition claims, and regional language callouts for quick shelf navigation.
Scalable architecture A flexible grid system adapts seamlessly across cartons, pouches, and PET bottles while maintaining a consistent brand identity.
Imagery Quick consumption, anytime-anywhere moments positioned within a health-first category — stronger marination achieved by styling hyper-realistic ingredients with vector-style hand-drawn illustration for a refreshing approach.
