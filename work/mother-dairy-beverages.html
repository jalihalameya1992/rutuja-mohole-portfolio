Mother Dairy Beverages

One packaging system for Mother Dairy's milkshakes, lassi and buttermilk range.

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Packaging Design + System Design
Category
Milkshakes, lassi, buttermilk

Task at hand

Create the packaging for Mother Dairy's beverages (milkshakes, lassi and buttermilk) as one design system that freshens up the range and keeps the trust and familiarity of the Mother Dairy name. It had to work across formats, flavours and occasions, speak to modern, health-conscious families, and still be recognised instantly in the aisle.

Challenge

Give indulgent milkshakes and traditional dairy drinks one look. Appeal to younger shoppers without putting off Mother Dairy's existing audience. Stay consistent across cartons, pouches and bottles while keeping each variant and category easy to tell apart, and balance appetite appeal with clear information in a fast-moving store.

04 / 09 the system

Design System

One system had to hold three quite different drinks (milkshakes, lassi, buttermilk) without losing the thread between them.

Different, but one family A shared pack structure ties Milkshakes, Lassi and Buttermilk to the Mother Dairy name, while each category keeps its own character.
Looks tasty Milk splashes and bright ingredient photography say fresh, indulgent and real flavour.
Easy to scan A clear information order: flavour cues, nutrition claims and regional-language callouts you can read quickly on shelf.
A grid that flexes A flexible grid fits cartons, pouches and PET bottles and keeps the brand consistent.
Photography Quick, anytime drinks in a health-first category. Hyper-realistic ingredients are paired with hand-drawn, vector-style illustration for a fresher look.
