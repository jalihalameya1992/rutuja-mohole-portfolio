PetStar

Packaging and system design launching Mankind Pharma's science-backed pet nutrition brand.

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Packaging design + System design
Category
Pet nutrition, FMCG

Task at hand

PetStar is Mankind Pharma's move into pet nutrition — the job was to make it feel like a credible, science-backed food brand for modern pet parents, not just another pharma line extension. The packaging needed a system that could scale cleanly across life stages (puppy, adult), flavours, and both dogs and cats.

Challenge

Walk the line between too clinical — which reads like medicine, not food — and too playful, which undercuts the nutritional credibility a pharma-backed brand should carry. And still fit a lot of real information on pack — life stage, protein content, digestive and coat benefits — without it turning into a wall of text next to legacy pet food giants already owning the shelf.

Pack structure — front, side and back panels, animated.

Brand mood: fun & dynamic, playful & approachable, expert & serious.

08 / 09 the system

Design System

Built to feel like a science-backed treat, not a prescription — vibrant enough to compete on shelf, credible enough to trust.

Color-coded life stages Blue, red and teal read apart at a glance — starter, puppy and adult each get their own lane on shelf.
Expressive pet photography Centered the pack around vibrant, vitality-driven imagery of real dogs, celebrating companionship rather than clinical function.
Scannable benefit icons Immunity, digestion, coat and life-stage benefits called out with clear icons, so the key facts read in a glance rather than a paragraph.
Ingredient credibility Functional botanicals like Ashwagandha and Silybum Marianum called out clearly on-pack, without tipping the design into clinical territory.
Retail extension The same system scaled cleanly onto billboards and in-mall standees, keeping shelf and street consistent.
