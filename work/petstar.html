PetStar

Packaging for Mankind Pharma's new, science-backed pet nutrition brand.

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Packaging design + System design
Category
Pet nutrition, FMCG

Task at hand

PetStar is Mankind Pharma's move into pet nutrition. The job was to make it feel like a credible, science-backed food brand for modern pet parents, not another pharma line extension. The packaging had to work across life stages (puppy, adult), flavours, and both dogs and cats.

Challenge

Too clinical and it reads like medicine, not food. Too playful and it undercuts the credibility a pharma-backed brand should have. And there's a lot of real information to fit on pack (life stage, protein content, digestive and coat benefits) without it becoming a wall of text next to the big pet food names that already own the shelf.

Pack structure: front, side and back panels, animated.

Brand mood: fun & dynamic, playful & approachable, expert & serious.

08 / 09 the system

Design System

Built to feel like a science-backed treat, not a prescription. Vibrant enough to compete on shelf, credible enough to trust.

A colour for each life stage Blue, red and teal read apart at a glance. Starter, puppy and adult each get their own lane on shelf.
Expressive pet photography The pack is built around lively photos of real dogs, showing companionship rather than clinical function.
Benefits at a glance Immunity, digestion, coat and life-stage benefits called out with clear icons, so the key facts read in a glance rather than a paragraph.
Real ingredients, up front Functional botanicals like Ashwagandha and Silybum Marianum called out clearly on-pack, without tipping the design into clinical territory.
Out in the world The same system scaled cleanly onto billboards and in-mall standees, keeping shelf and street consistent.
