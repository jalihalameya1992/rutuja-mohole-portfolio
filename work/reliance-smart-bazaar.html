Reliance Smart Bazaar

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Identity Design + Visual System Guidelines
Scale
1,000+ retail stores, bilingual signage

Task at hand

Refresh the identity and signage system for Reliance Smart Bazaar to create a cohesive, scalable brand experience across 1,000+ stores. The objective was to improve legibility, strengthen brand recognition, and build a flexible visual system that performs consistently across physical and digital touchpoints — without losing existing brand equity.

Challenge

Build a scalable identity system that adapts across diverse store formats, architectural conditions, and regional regulations. Improve visibility and readability while retaining familiarity of the existing brand, and create a unified signage framework that seamlessly accommodates both English and regional-language logos — balancing flexibility with consistency across thousands of real-world retail applications.

Brand system overview — animated.

07 / 07 the system

Design System

Less a redesign, more a tightening — same identity, just sharper, and built to hold up on a storefront in any city, in any script.

Refined identity Sharpened the existing logo through improved proportions, spacing, and visual balance to enhance legibility without changing its core identity.
Adaptive fascia logic A modular signage system intelligently responds to different storefront sizes and formats while maintaining a consistent brand presence.
Signature blue band A strong blue fascia band acts as a constant visual anchor, creating immediate recognition across stores.
Bilingual typography system Standardized typography, proportions, and hierarchy across English and regional scripts, crafted with a type designer specializing in regional scripts.
Scalable brand architecture Comprehensive guidelines covering facades, storefronts, vehicles, packaging, digital media, and retail communications ensure consistency across every touchpoint.
