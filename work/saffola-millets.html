Saffola Millets Masala

Branding, packaging and photography direction for Saffola's push into millet snacking.

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Branding + Packaging + Photography Direction
Category
Millet snacking, FMCG

Task at hand

Saffola is known for health; it's been India's #1 oats brand for years. When they decided to get into millets too, the job was to take a grain most people think of as bland diet food or something their grandmother cooked, and turn it into a 3-minute snack families would actually reach for.

Challenge

Outside South India, most people don't really know what to do with millets. They assume they take forever to cook, taste bland, or both. The pack had to make you hungry just looking at it, sell "quick" as hard as it sold "healthy," and still feel unmistakably like Saffola.

Campus AR activation and metro OOH extension.

01 / 09 the system

Design System

Built to make millets feel less like diet food you're obligated to eat, and more like something you'd actually reach for on a hungry evening.

Making millets exciting Reframed coarse ancient grains from slow diet food into an exciting 3-minute impulse snack.
Quick and easy, on pack Subtle farm-to-table grain accents sit alongside clean, hand-drawn icons for "Quick & Easy", "Ready in 3 mins" and a spoon for convenience. The benefits are upfront and easy to scan.
Two-colour flavour coding A diagonal half-and-half layout: Saffola's signature yellow for brand recall, plus bold flavour-led colour blocking to stand out on shelf.
Close-up food photography Close-up food photography of rich masala millets overflowing the bowl against Saffola's golden yellow backdrop.
The heart-shaped bowl The dish is served in a custom heart-shaped bowl, turning Saffola's heart logo into something that makes you hungry.
Showing how it's eaten A hand dips a spoon into the bowl to spark the craving and show how quick it is to eat.
