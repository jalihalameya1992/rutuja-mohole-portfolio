Saffola Millets Masala

Branding, packaging and photography direction for Saffola's push into millet snacking.

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Branding + Packaging + Photography direction
Category
Millet snacking, FMCG

Task at hand

Saffola's known for health — it's been India's #1 oats brand for years. When they decided to get into millets too, the job was to take a grain most people think of as bland diet food or something their grandmother cooked, and turn it into a 3-minute snack families would actually reach for.

Challenge

Outside South India, most people don't really know what to do with millets — they assume it takes forever to cook, tastes bland, or both. The pack had to make you hungry just looking at it, sell "quick" as hard as it sold "healthy," and still feel unmistakably like Saffola.

Packaging brought to life across digital touchpoints — animated.

Campus AR activation and metro OOH extension.

01 / 09 the system

Design System

Built to make millets feel less like diet food you're obligated to eat, and more like something you'd actually reach for on a hungry evening.

De-stigmatizing millets Re-framed coarse ancient grains from a slow diet food into an exciting, 3-minute "anytime" impulse snack.
Convenience cues Combined subtle farm-to-table grain accents with hand-drawn, clean icons highlighting "Quick & Easy", "Ready in 3 mins", and a spoon for convenience — upfront, clean, scannable benefits.
Dual flavor coding Used a diagonal half-and-half layout — signature Saffola yellow for masterbrand recall and bold flavor-inspired color blocking for maximum shelf throw.
Macro hunger photography Styled macro food photography featuring rich masala millets overflowing in Saffola's signature golden yellow backdrop.
Heart-equity bowl Served the dish in a custom, iconic heart-shaped bowl — turning Saffola's trusted masterbrand heart logo into an appetite-driven visual anchor.
Humanized consumption codes Depicted a hand dipping into the bowl with a spoon to trigger instant craving and signal rapid consumption.
