ITC Sunfeast Marie

A fresh pack for one of India's biggest biscuit brands.

Elephant Design, Pune

Role
Identity + Packaging Design
Category
Marie Light: Active, Oats Fibre, Vita Orange, Veda

Task at hand

Modernise Sunfeast Marie Light, from the house of ITC and one of India's biggest household biscuit brands. The job was to refresh the whole Marie Light range (Active, Oats Fibre, Vita Orange and Veda) so it stays relevant to health-conscious families while shoppers still recognise it instantly in the aisle.

Challenge

Changing the visual anchors on a huge legacy biscuit risks confusing millions of daily shoppers in crowded general trade stores. The pack also had to communicate wellness and set apart functional sub-brands like Sunfeast Veda Marie Light, which has 5 natural Ayurvedic ingredients (Ginger, Tulsi, Mulethi, Cardamom, Ashwagandha), without feeling clinical or pharmaceutical.

06 / 09 the system

Design System

The hard part wasn't designing something new. It was changing a biscuit pack millions of people buy on autopilot without anyone noticing.

Keeping what people recognise Kept the trusted Sunfeast sunshine and warm golden yellows, with engraved biscuits that show the moment of eating.
Decluttering the grid Reworked the pack structure into a brand unit Sunfeast can own, with organised claims and appetising food shots.
The farm story A central brand unit with placards for variant call-outs, and a soft gradient behind the Sunfeast logo that hints at the farm-to-table story.
Tea-time imagery Realistic compositions of steaming tea cups, natural ingredients and crisp golden biscuits bring out the comfort of the ritual.
Veda Marie Detailed biscuit renders mixed with spices, which is unusual in the category, plus a sub-brand roundel and a bright yellow and natural green palette that stands out from the clutter.
